Snake Arena Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: August 13, 2026

How to Request Data Deletion

If you have used Snake Arena and would like to request deletion of personal information associated with your use of the game, you may submit a deletion request to the Snake Arena development team.

What to Include

Your request should include enough information for us to identify the relevant account or information. Please do not send passwords, payment information, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Facebook or Meta Login

If you accessed Snake Arena through Facebook or another Meta service, you can also manage or remove the application's access through your Facebook account settings.

Deletion Process

After receiving a valid deletion request, we will review the request and delete applicable personal information that we control, subject to legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate retention requirements.

Game Data

Some gameplay information may be stored only temporarily or may not be associated with an identifiable person. Where applicable, such information may not require a separate deletion request.

Contact

To request deletion of your information, contact the Snake Arena development team through the contact method provided with the game or its official platform.

Privacy Policy

For more information about how Snake Arena handles information, please review our Privacy Policy.
